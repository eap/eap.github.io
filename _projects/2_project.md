In the summer of 2025 I went on a hike with my brother to Blue Lake in the Indian Peaks
Wilderness. We tried to get to Little Blue Lake but took an unmaintained rock scrambling
trail that was marked on AllTrails and ended up in a dangerous position; exhausted and in
technical terrain that we wisely navigated out of. Looking into that I found out that the
trail was an OSM way, unofficial and annotated as a "user path" with an approximate SAC T5
rating. This was added from OSM then the annotations were hidden because... I guess
because they don't want users to know that the data is raided from an open source project.
From that day I've been thinking of how to build an alternative... and then AI and agentic
coding became a thing!

So [EvanTopo](https://evantopo.com/) is kind of two experiments. Can I do something in my
spare time on the side that should by all means take several years of old school effort?
And can I do better than the alternatives; no reverse engineering, but actually make
something that is the better choice.
