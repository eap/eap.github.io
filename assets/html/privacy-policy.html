Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2025-April-22
App Name: FatWallet (com.eap.wallet)

This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when you use FatWallet (“the App”).

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information through the App itself. The App operates entirely offline and does not transmit or sync any data to external servers.

However, due to distribution through the Google Play Store, certain minimal demographic and purchase-related information may be shared with us.

Local Data Storage

All data you enter into the App (e.g., card images, labels, or notes) is stored locally on your device.
We do not have access to this data. It is never transmitted, uploaded, or shared unless you explicitly do so.

App Permissions

The App may request access to:

  • Camera/Storage – Used only to add or display images (e.g., card scans).

These permissions are required solely to support basic app functionality. They are not used for tracking or advertising.

Children’s Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Purchases and demographic collection via Google Play are managed by Google and subject to their parental controls and policies.
